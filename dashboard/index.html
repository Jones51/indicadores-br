How each number is calculated
  • Selic target: the Selic policy rate target set by Copom and in force on the last day of the month (SGS 432), in % per year.
  • IPCA 12 months: official inflation accumulated over 12 months (SGS 13522). A month's IPCA is only released the following month, so the most recent month may not have inflation yet.
  • Monthly IPCA: the month's change in consumer prices (SGS 433). Months not yet released have no bar (they are not zero).
  • Inflation target and band: set by the National Monetary Council (CMN) for each year — for example 4.50% ± 2.0 pp until 2016 and 3.00% ± 1.5 pp since 2024 (official history at the Central Bank). Since 2025 the target is continuous: 3.00% ± 1.5 pp for 12-month IPCA, checked every month, until the CMN sets another one. Until 2024 the target applied to the calendar-year (December) IPCA, so the position of other months against the band is a reference, not a formal miss.
  • IPCA 12m vs. band: above the ceiling (target + tolerance), within the band (limits included) or below the floor (target − tolerance), computed in the dbt model.
  • Ex-post real rate: Selic target deflated by 12-month IPCA using the Fisher equation: (1 + Selic) ÷ (1 + IPCA 12m) − 1.
  • USD/BRL PTAX: average of the month's daily selling rates (SGS 1). In the current month, the average so far.
  • Cards: each shows the latest month in which that indicator has a value and the change versus the same month a year earlier — in percentage points (pp) for rates and in % for the dollar. The small trend line shows the 24 months up to that month, with the latest one marked; it does not follow the chart period.
  • Key alerts (always on the latest data, regardless of the chart period):
    • Inflation outside the band (warning): the latest 12-month IPCA is above the ceiling or below the floor. It becomes critical from 6 consecutive months outside the band counted since January 2025, the continuous-target definition of a missed target.
    • Copom decision (info): the Selic target at the end of the latest month differs from the previous month's.
    • Large dollar move: the monthly average PTAX changed 5% or more versus the previous month — a warning when the dollar rises, info when it falls.
    • When no rule fires, the strip says “No alerts”.
  • Colors: series keep one color in every chart (IPCA is always orange). In the cards only inflation and the dollar changes are colored — red when they rise, green when they fall; Selic and the real rate stay neutral. Status colors (green within the band, amber warning, red critical) always come with an icon and a text label.
